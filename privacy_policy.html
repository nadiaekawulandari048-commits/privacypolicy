Privacy Policy

Seal Downloader (com.videotools.sealdownloaderapp)
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Seal Downloader ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

Summary: Seal Downloader does NOT require account registration, does NOT ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or GPS location, and does NOT sell any personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in user privacy and data minimization. When using Seal Downloader:

  • We do not require user registration or logins.
  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • We do not access personal media files, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.
  • Your playlists, bookmarks, and viewing history are stored strictly locally on your own device and are never uploaded to any server.

2. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

To support the development and maintenance of Seal Downloader, the Application integrates reputable third-party SDKs for advertising and analytics. These providers may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), device model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Network Information: IP address (used for approximate country-level location), network connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • Ad Interaction Data: Views, clicks, and engagement with advertisements displayed within the app.

Below are the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used in the Application:

  • Google Play Services / Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AppLovin MAX: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/

3. How Information Is Used

The technical and advertising data collected by third-party services is used solely to:

  • Deliver personalized and non-personalized advertisements within the Application.
  • Monitor ad impressions, fraud prevention, and performance analytics.
  • Ensure optimal streaming performance and stability across different Android devices.

4. Advertising ID & Opt-Out Options

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Open Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since Seal Downloader does not collect personal accounts or server-side user profiles, we do not store your personal data on remote servers. All local preferences, custom playlists, and watch histories can be erased at any time simply by clearing the Application's storage via your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Seal Downloader does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through a third-party partner, please contact us immediately so we can coordinate the prompt removal of such information.

7. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. We use commercially acceptable methods and industry-standard security protocols to ensure that data communication (such as remote configuration checks) is transmitted over secure HTTPS channels.
